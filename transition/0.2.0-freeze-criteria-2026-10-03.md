## What the freeze does prove

The 0.2.0 freeze means:

- the current authoring contract is stable enough for systematic content
  development;
- the bounded semantic questions above have deterministic dispositions;
- maintained schema/semantic/compiler/migration regressions pass at the recorded
  checkpoint;
- Self-Assertion and current NIWC migration/integration gates remain green at
  the recorded checkpoint;
- the project has a documented authority hierarchy and handoff process.

## What the freeze does not prove

It does **not** mean:

- every SCAP-NG capability has standalone conformance content;
- every capability has live collector/target execution evidence;
- independent scanners have demonstrated equivalent results;
- the complete #128 corpus is finished;
- final signatures/trust or privacy profiles are complete;
- ESX or Kubernetes new Tests are standardized;
- the future editor exists;
- the draft is Board-ratified or a published external standard.

Those claims require stronger conformance evidence layers.
