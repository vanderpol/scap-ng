A generated evaluation/provenance display must explicitly distinguish `complete`, `incomplete`, `error`, `not_collected`, `not_applicable`, and `does_not_exist`.

Do not replace two State predicates with a range without a six-state/multiplicity proof; the inherited [truth-table unit tests](../../../tools/test_oval_result_truth_tables.py) are now added to the research CI.

Adding blanket required schema fields by default or a `nullable` alias would hide rather than resolve data acquisition semantics.
4. **Repeated skeptic:** no concrete new lossless-safe syntax deletion emerges from the accessible Gemini output. Retain the five source-invertible files; revisit further grammar change only on a verified source-backed counterexample.
