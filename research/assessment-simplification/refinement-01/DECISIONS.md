# Experimental decision candidates, version 2

Unpublished research candidates, superseding only the investigative evidence in
[version 1](../DECISIONS.md). Neither version is a ratified decision or a parallel
review tree. No published Discussion is edited and no Board vote is opened.
Promotion requires coordination through the sole `review/current/` surface.

Each candidate SHALL be one separate versioned yes/no GitHub Discussion if
promoted. Opening-post 👍 means Yes and 👎 means No; comments explain rationale.
Eligibility, quorum and recorded disposition still apply. Reactions alone do
not establish ratification.

These are proposed normative propositions; no owner decision is required to
continue the bounded tests. A Board SHALL resolve core language changes before
they are treated as established requirements. Scanner implementers and authors
SHOULD assess collector cost and readability separately from YAML size.

Retain the version 1 rejected approaches. Refinement additionally rejects global
existence for a matrix of required paths, predicate-based identity deduplication,
optional-uncollected as absent, collection errors encoded as zero in a new typed
contract, and atomic-snapshot claims based solely on a session GUID.

No capability retirement proposal is supported. Rare non-deprecated SCAP1.4
validation features remain required. Deprecated Tests SHALL remain unsupported;
schema/governance replacement guidance belongs in source remediation evidence.
