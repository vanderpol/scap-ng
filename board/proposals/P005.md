# [VOTE P005] Deprecated source Test exclusion

## Yes/no question

**Do you agree with the following proposed decision?**

> Effectively deprecated OVAL Tests SHALL block automated NG conversion. Later authoritative reinstatements SHALL be explicitly recorded; replacement Tests SHALL require validated source remediation rather than automatic substitution.

React to the **Discussion's opening post**: 👍 **Yes**, 👎 **No**. Comments may clarify or explain a vote. They do not replace the yes/no question. Vote on this version; a substantive revision requires a new version and a fresh vote. Eligibility, quorum, voting window and final disposition must be approved before reactions can be treated as an official Board decision. No such approval is inferred here.

## Rationale and current status

This preserves the owner requirement and the governance override exception. A verified manual procedure may remain available without claiming automated equivalence.

This proposal may ratify a working project direction or resolve an open historical question. Implementation and schema validation are evidence, not Board approval. A yes reaction agrees with this specific proposition, not every historical detail in the sources.

## Alternative and compatibility impact

A no reaction rejects this proposition for the initial NG baseline. It does not authorize silent removal of existing semantics or restoration of a superseded architecture. An alternative should identify the concrete required behavior and its authoring, migration, validation and runtime costs. Approved changes require specification/crosswalk updates and focused conformance tests before implementation claims.
